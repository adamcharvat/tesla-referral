FAQ

Before you click

What exactly do I get through the referral link?

When you order a new Model 3 or Model Y through this link, you get 1,000 km of free Supercharging. The exact reward follows Tesla's current terms for your region.

Does the car price change?

No. Tesla sells at fixed prices, directly, with no dealers — the referral link never changes what you pay. The reward is a bonus on top.

When do I need to use the link?

Before you complete your order. A referral cannot be added retroactively after an order is placed — so click the link before you start configuring.

Which Superchargers does the credit work at?

At all Tesla Superchargers at home and abroad. Kilometres are deducted based on your car's consumption.

When is the credit applied?

After you take delivery of the car. Until then it usually shows as “pending” in your Tesla account and activates in the app after delivery.

How many referrals can an owner earn per year?

Tesla caps referral rewards at 10 successful referrals per calendar year per account.
